      // 01 INTEL

      What the Eye sees

      It looks like a forbidden cockpit. It is also a memecoin. Both halves are the magic. Everything below is real, public and live.

      • 01

        Live aircraft and a real cockpit

        Thousands of real ADS-B transponders from the OpenSky Network, refreshed every minute and dead-reckoned in between. Click one and see what it is: type, registration, operator, route and a photo of the actual airframe from Planespotters.net. Follow it, or ride in the cockpit: speed, heading and altitude readouts, a briefing strip with the place below you, live local weather, nearby signals and regional headlines, and opt-in WX clouds built from actual observations.

      • 02

        23,000+ public cameras

        Traffic and weather cameras across North America (Florida, Georgia, Pennsylvania, Connecticut, Arizona, Nevada, Idaho, Louisiana, Alaska, Oregon, Austin, Ontario, Alberta, British Columbia, Yukon, Calgary), Europe (London, Norway, Finland, Estonia, Tallinn and 400 alpine weather cams in Austria, Germany, Italy and Switzerland) and Asia-Pacific (Hong Kong, Singapore, Sydney, New Zealand). Open one, fly down, watch it refresh. With a free Windy key the WEBCAMS HERE button pulls in tens of thousands more on every continent, Africa and Asia included.

      • 03

        Satellites in orbit

        The ISS, Tiangong, Hubble and the brightest objects in orbit, propagated live from CelesTrak orbital elements.

      • 04

        Earthquakes, radar, cables

        USGS earthquakes from the last 24 hours, RainViewer weather radar and the global submarine cable map, one switch each.

      • 05

        Photoreal globe, day and night

        Real satellite imagery with borders and place names, a live day-night terminator and VIIRS city lights on the dark side. 3D, top-down or flat map. Add a free Cesium ion token for terrain and 3D buildings.

      • 06

        Reskin reality

        Electro-optical, FLIR thermal, night vision, CRT. Your portfolio imaged in every spectrum. Still red in all of them.

      • 07

        Routes and flythroughs

        Switch on DRAW, click waypoints on the globe and the route snaps to real roads or footpaths (OSRM on OpenStreetMap). FLYTHROUGH then flies the camera along it at street height. DETECT paints screen-space boxes and IDs on every contact in frame.

      • 08

        Street level

        Hit STREET VIEW and click the spot you want to stand on, double-click anywhere, or open it from a camera's window: Google Street View opens right there and you can walk the streets from orbit.

      • 09

        Search and share

        Type coordinates or a place name to fly there. Hit SHARE and the camera, layers and tracked target serialize into a link.

      • 10

        Hidden Eyes hunt

        Ten eyes are hidden on the globe, on cameras, satellites and aircraft. Connect a wallet, find them, climb the live leaderboard; the first operators to find all ten are paid in SOL automatically.

      • 11

        No bag left behind

        Every whale, every rug, every jeet. Tracked from 560 km up. $EYE is the ticker of the watchers.
